EPoSe

Prove service. Earn QWC.

EPoSe rewards registered Service Nodes for proven network services. The protocol evaluates valid attestations recorded on the blockchain and determines which nodes qualify for reward rotation.

01

Provide service.

Registered Service Nodes provide network services during each epoch.

02

Verify service.

Protocol-selected verifiers check service responses and submit signed attestations.

03

Receive rewards.

After qualification is finalized, eligible Service Nodes enter the protocol's deterministic reward rotation.

EPoSe origin

Thank you, Xecute.

Xecute developed the original EPoSe vision and contributed the Qwertycoin service-layer whitepaper. That work laid the foundation for rewarding useful network services.

Rewards follow the protocol. Every full node can verify reward eligibility and recipient selection from the blockchain. Subsidy allocation: 90% mining / 10% EPoSe service rewards; transaction fees remain with the miner.

EPoSe FAQ

Short answers for new operators.

What is Qwertycoin?

Qwertycoin (QWC) is a privacy-focused cryptocurrency with RandomX proof-of-work mining and EPoSe rewards for proven network services.

How do I use the Web Wallet?

Open the official Web Wallet, create or restore a wallet there and follow its safety instructions. The main website never processes wallet keys.

Can I use an old QWC wallet?

No. The current QWC network is separate from the earlier chain. Legacy wallet software and blockchain data are not compatible with this network.

Does EPoSe replace mining?

No. RandomX miners produce blocks and secure the blockchain. EPoSe adds rewards for proven network services.

What is an epoch?

An epoch is the block period used to assess EPoSe service activity. It currently spans 720 blocks, approximately 24 hours at the target block interval.

What does qualified mean?

A qualified Service Node has met the protocol's requirements for valid attestations in the relevant epoch and is eligible for service-reward selection. The technical quorum is at least ceil(2 x actual committee size / 3).

Who determines qualification and rewards?

The EPoSe protocol. Every full node applies the same rules to valid on-chain registrations and attestations to verify eligibility and reward selection.

Do Service Nodes need to stake QWC?

No. Participation requires EPoSe registration and the required service proofs, not locked QWC as stake.

Can one operator run multiple Service Nodes?

Yes. Each Service Node needs its own valid registration and must meet the protocol's service requirements. Admission proof makes cheap identity creation harder.

Why is EPoSe not Proof of Stake?

EPoSe rewards proven service rather than locked stake. Service Nodes verify blockchain data like other full nodes, while RandomX miners perform proof-of-work block production.
